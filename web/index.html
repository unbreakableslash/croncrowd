Model assumptions & schedule notes

Five-field cron with Vixie day-of-month/weekday rules. DST gaps skip starts; repeated wall minutes match twice. Scheduler policies may differ. Overload means modeled resource demand exceeds capacity, not a measured production incident. Work already running at the start is included.
